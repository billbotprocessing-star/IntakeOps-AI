# 💄 Med-Spa & Aesthetics Intake Blueprint

## Phase 1: Service Interest
Identify if the caller is interested in:
* **Injectables** (Botox/Fillers)
* **Skin** (Facials/Lasers)
* **Body** (Contouring/CoolSculpting)

## Phase 2: Medical Pre-Screening
1. "Are you currently pregnant or breastfeeding?" (Disqualifier for most services).
2. "Have you had this specific treatment before?" (Determines if they need a 'New Patient Consultation').

## Phase 3: Booking & Deposit
* **Rule:** The AI must mention the "No-Show Policy."
* **Action:** "I can hold that Friday at 2:00 PM for you. You will receive a text link to pay the $50 booking deposit. This will be applied toward your treatment."

## Phase 4: Action Logic
* **Booked:** Sync appointment to the booking software (e.g., JaneApp, Boulevard).
* **Inquiry Only:** Add to the "Email Nurture" list for monthly specials.
