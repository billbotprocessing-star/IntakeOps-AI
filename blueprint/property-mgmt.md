# 🏢 Property Management Intake Blueprint

## Phase 1: Identity & Routing
* **Tenant:** Filter for Maintenance vs. Lease questions.
* **Prospect:** Filter by Property Interest & Move-in Date.
* **Owner:** High-Priority routing to the Management team.

## Phase 2: Maintenance Triage
If a Tenant calls with a repair request, the AI must ask:
1. "Is there an active leak, fire, or lack of heat?" (**Emergency**)
2. "Do you have pets that need to be secured?" (For technician safety)
3. "Can we have permission to enter if you aren't home?"

## Phase 3: Action Logic
* **Maintenance:** Create a ticket in the CRM and send an SMS confirmation.
* **Leasing:** Send a link to the digital application if they meet the "No Pets" or "Income" criteria.
* **Owner:** Transfer call directly to the Business Development Manager.
