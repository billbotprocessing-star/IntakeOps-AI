# 🛡 AI Guardrails & Safety

To ensure professional service, IntakeOps AI agents are governed by the following hard-coded rules:

1. **No Pricing Guarantees:** Agents may only provide "starting at" estimates. Final quotes must be given by a human.
2. **No Legal/Medical Advice:** Agents are strictly intake specialists. They cannot interpret law or diagnose health issues.
3. **Discharge Disclosure:** The AI must state the call is recorded for quality purposes where required by law.
4. **PII Scrubbing:** Any sensitive data (SSNs, Credit Cards) is redacted from transcripts before being stored.
