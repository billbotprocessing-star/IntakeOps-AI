# ⚔️ Sales Battle Cards

### Objection: "I prefer a human receptionist."
**Response:** "Humans sleep, get sick, and take lunches. IntakeOps is 24/7, never misses a call, and costs 90% less than a full-time hire."

### Objection: "AI sounds robotic."
**Response:** "We use high-fidelity neural voices. Most callers don't realize it's AI until they are already being scheduled. Would you rather have a 'robotic' call that books a job, or a human voicemail that loses one?"

### Objection: "Is my data safe?"
**Response:** "We use SOC2 compliant infrastructure and end-to-end encryption. Your lead data goes straight to your CRM, nowhere else."
